# Building With Github

1. Fork the repository.
2. Click Actions.
3. Click Build.
4. On the right side, click the arrow beside `Run workflow`.
![[Pasted image 20260225133906.png]]
5.  Add the link to the uploaded decrypted IPA
6.  Click `Enter` or scroll down and click the green `Run workflow`.
7. Wait for it to finish building.
![[Pasted image 20260225133607.png]]

8. Delete you forked repository after building so Google would not serve you DMCA.
