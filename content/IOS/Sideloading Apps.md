
# Impactor

1. Install Plume Impactor from GitHub.
2. Open Plume Impactor
3. Log in with your Apple ID credentials.
4. Drag your IPA file to Impactor.
5. Check some things
6. Edit App Icon and Name (optional)
7. Connect you IPhone to your laptop / pc.
8. Press Install.
9. Do not disconnect! Wait until it says 100%.
