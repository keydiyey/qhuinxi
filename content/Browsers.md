### Extensions

These are the some of the main extensions to remove ads from your browser

- **[UBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com/)**     Blocks ads <3
- **SponsorBlock**         Automatically  skips sponsor contents in youtube
- **[ClearURLs](https://docs.clearurls.xyz/1.23.0/#download)**           Removes tracking from links
- **TamperMonkey**      Patches your browser with userscripts
- **Indie Wiki Buddy**   Changes *fandom.wiki* links to independent ones
