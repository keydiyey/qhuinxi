I want to increase my typing speed and accuracy. I make quite a lot of mistakes when typing words and often find myself deleting several letters to correct it.  This takes up much of my time. 

While searching for weird or functional websites to add to my collection, I came across [`keybr`](https://keybr.com). It trains you to type the most common letters using the most common words. You unlock a letter once you reach a certain WPM (words per minute). The site calculates your average WPM and accuracy.

![[Pasted image 20260223220648.png]]

My starting speed was around 54 WPM whereas my starting accuracy was at 96.79%.  My goal is to reach an average of 80 WPM while still maintaining high accuracy. After two days of usage, my average WPM went up at around 60.
