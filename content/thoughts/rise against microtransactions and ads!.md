
Ads... Ads... everywhere!

Whatever I do, ads will pop up. Walking down towards my favorite spot (7-11), I see a big glowing signage of Chowking almost replacing the sun in a background of a serene blue sky. I walk back home, tarpaulins of cigarettes are lined up. After a long hard day of studying in class, you think you deserve funny cat videos on the internet. As you turn on YouTube you are greeted with a pop-up.

> Enhanced Video Quality with YouTube Premium! Ad Free

I think to myself, "Well, I have a small screen so I do not need super 4k HD quality" and "It is just a single ad". I was wrong. At a young age of 12, I was radicalized by Google. Is it just me or is there such a huge downgrade within the quality of videos online. The 720p that used to be clear is made up of 4 pixels now. There is not just one ad. There are ads within ads, and these ads are a minute long. 

And so I closed YouTube and try to stream music with Spotify. 

> Want a break from the ads? Tap the banner now....

Nope. As I listened to this elevator pitch of Spotify about why I should buy their premium, all I felt was annoyance. I did not subscribe. I was a student with zero coins in my piggy bank. They revoked my rights to dumb shuffling, access to lyrics, downloads, and skips. I could always go back to downloading mp3, but having a bunch of discography ready and music recommendations seemed so good to me.

I hurriedly searched "How to get premium Spotify for free?" and "How remove YouTube ads without Premium?". A bunch of sites are offering different solutions. I fell down the rabbit hole of cracked applications and rooting android phones. 

Currently, I am using a bruised iPhone XR. Long gone are my android days, but oh, how I miss them. I ran into the same problems but worse. A lot of ads, a lot of paywalls. You cannot even install a cracked app without going through hoops and loops. Furthermore, Apple lets you sign a sideloaded application only for a week (unless you pay for a developer license) and so you need to resign weekly. Google shuts down every YouTube IPA distribution and people can't just download a fully tweaked app that easily. As for me, I managed to build one using a [tutorial](https://github.com/arichornlover/uYouEnhanced/wiki/Building) made by **@arichornlover**. It is a fork of a fork of [[UYouEnhanced]].  Basically, It automatically tweaks your IPA as long as you have the file. I am planning to check other tweaks but for now I will savor this moment of seven days ad-free joy.
